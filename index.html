Welcome

I am an economist working on macroeconomics, monetary and fiscal policy, financial frictions, and the economies of Russia and Central Asia. My academic research focuses especially on the collateral and liquidity roles of government debt and their implications for monetary–fiscal policy.

Research Interests

  • Monetary–fiscal interactions
  • Government debt and collateral
  • Financial frictions
  • Macro-prudential fiscal policy
  • Unconventional monetary policy
  • Liquidity and convenience yields
  • Russian economy
  • Central Asian economies

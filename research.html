Research

Optimal Monetary and Fiscal Policy Rules under Debt Collateralization

Job Market Paper

This paper studies optimal monetary and fiscal policy rules when government debt and capital serve as collateral in financial markets. When borrowing constraints tighten, collateral premiums distort investment and production. The paper examines how monetary policy should respond to these financial conditions and finds that optimal policy actively eases when collateral premiums rise.

Optimal Supply of Government Debt: Collateralization and Overaccumulation of Capital

Working Paper

Capital can substitute for government debt in collateral markets. When the collateral premium is high, firms may overaccumulate capital to relax borrowing constraints. Government debt can crowd out this inefficient capital demand while also creating fiscal costs through debt service and distortionary taxation.
